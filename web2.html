Cyber Security.

Cyberattacks are common today. Learn how to stay safe online, identify common threats, and protect your personal information.

What is Cyber Security?

Cyber security is the practice of protecting computers, mobile devices, networks, applications, and personal information from unauthorized access and harmful activities.

The internet is an important part of everyday life. We use it for communication, education, entertainment, shopping, banking, and many other activities.

Because so much information is shared online, it is important to understand common cyber threats and learn how to use the internet safely.

Stay Safe Online

A few simple habits can help protect you while using the internet.

🔐 Use Strong Passwords Create strong, unique passwords and avoid sharing them with others.
🎣 Watch for Phishing Be careful with suspicious emails, messages, websites, and links.
🛡️ Keep Devices Updated Install security and software updates when they become available.
👤 Protect Personal Information Avoid sharing sensitive personal information with unknown people online.
