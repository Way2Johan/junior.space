Secure connection, eavesdropping & phishing.

Three ways your data or identity can be exposed online — and how to recognize each one.

Foundation

What is a secure connection

A secure connection is a connection encrypted by one or more protocols to ensure the security of data flowing between two or more nodes. When a connection isn't encrypted, it can be easily hacked or listened to by anyone with the knowledge of how to do it.

Silent threat

What is eavesdropping

Eavesdropping is the unauthorized real-time interception of a private communication, such as a phone call, instant messages, videoconference, or fax transmission. The term is derived from the practice of standing under the eaves of a house to listen to conversations inside. Eavesdropping attacks are difficult to detect because they don't cause network transmissions to appear abnormal.

Social engineering

What is phishing

While there are many online scams, phishing is geared specifically toward retrieving someone's personal information and using it to harm them. An identity thief, known as a phisher, lures victims using emails and websites that seem harmless or secure.

Fake emails are another common way phishing happens — always double-check the sender before clicking a link.
